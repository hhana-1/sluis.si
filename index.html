Questions

Do we have to change our web app?

No. Your web app keeps running as it is. The native apps use the same backend and APIs, so accounts and data are shared from the first day.

What does the AI do, and what do people do?

The AI translates each screen and component into SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose. Engineers review every screen, build the native features and test on real devices before anything reaches a store.

Which web stacks can you translate?

Most modern ones: React, Next.js, Vue, Nuxt, Angular, Svelte and server-rendered apps. The audit tells you exactly how much of yours translates directly.

Who owns the code?

You do. You get both repositories, and the apps are published under your own Apple and Google developer accounts.

Will Apple and Google approve the apps?

Nobody can promise an approval, but translated apps are native apps, not repackaged websites. We prepare the listing, submit the build and answer reviewers until it is approved.

What happens when our web app changes?

Store-Ready includes 30 days of fixes after launch. With Native Pro we translate your web changes every month and ship them through review.

How long does it take?

The audit takes five working days. Store-Ready apps usually reach the stores in 4 to 6 weeks, Native Pro apps in 8 to 12.

What about payments?

Physical goods and services, like a class or a delivery, keep your own checkout. Digital goods sold inside an app go through Apple’s and Google’s billing. The audit tells you which rules apply to you.
